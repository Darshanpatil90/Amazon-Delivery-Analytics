
# Category-wise Delivery Time Analysis

## Objective
To compare Delivery_Time across product categories and investigate
the significantly lower values observed in the Grocery category.

## Grocery Statistics

- Total Records: 2691
- Mean: 26.54
- Median: 26
- Standard Deviation: 9.49
- Minimum: 10
- Maximum: 54

## Key Observation

The Grocery category has a mean Delivery_Time of 26.54,
while other categories have mean values approximately between 129 and 133.

This indicates a significant difference in delivery time values.

## Conclusion

The reason for the lower Grocery delivery times cannot be confirmed
from the available dataset alone.

The unit of Delivery_Time is not explicitly documented.
Further clarification from the project lead is required.

No records were modified or removed during this analysis.
